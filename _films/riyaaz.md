##### Summary
An experimental documentary in which the personal philosophies, musings, and points of view of four renowned Indian Classical musicians at the Ali Akbar College of Music in San Rafael, CA interleave and accompany a roaming camera looking to explore both the musicians' own spaces, and the world around them.
